Why is this project useful?

The EBU have produced and published version 1 of their Audio Definition Model as Tech 3364. This model contains many Audio-related parameters than can be used across the wider professional audio industry. This project will study the EBU model and produce a version suitable for inclusion in the AES60 standard with amendments or additions if required to meet industries supported by the AES. Maintaining a common metadata for use across audio applications and processes would reduce the risk of incompatibility issues and improve general interoperability. Building on the existing work of AES60 and ensuring this work is fed back to the EBU would also promote wider industry interoperability.
